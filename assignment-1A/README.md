# Part 2 – Prompt Engineering

## Scenario 1 – The Budget Trip

### Role
Act as an experienced travel planner.

### Context
I have PKR 15,000 and 3 free days to travel with two friends in Pakistan without asking my parents for extra money.

### Main
Suggest a destination, transportation, accommodation, food options, and activities that fit within my budget.

### Conclusion
Provide a simple day-by-day itinerary with estimated costs in a clear and practical format.

---

## Scenario 2 – The Difficult Message

### Role
Act as a professional communication expert.

### Context
My landlord has ignored my requests to repair the water heater for two weeks. I need it fixed before winter while maintaining a good relationship.

### Main
Write a firm but respectful WhatsApp message requesting that the repair be completed as soon as possible.

### Conclusion
Keep the message polite, professional, and concise.

---

## Scenario 3 – The Impossible Schedule

### Role
Act as a productivity coach.

### Context
I am a 6th-semester student with five courses, a part-time job (15 hours/week), a side project due in three weeks, and I want to start going to the gym four times a week.

### Main
Create a realistic weekly timetable that balances study, work, gym, and rest while considering my energy levels.

### Conclusion
Present the timetable in a simple table and include a few productivity tips.

---

## Scenario 4 – The Broke Student Meal Plan

### Role
Act as a student nutritionist.

### Context
I have PKR 8,000 per month for food and cook in a hostel using only a stove and a small refrigerator.

### Main
Prepare a healthy 7-day meal plan with a grocery list that stays within my budget.

### Conclusion
Present the meal plan in a table with estimated grocery costs.

---

## Scenario 5 – The Scholarship Interview

### Role
Act as a scholarship interview coach.

### Context
My scholarship interview is in five days, and I become nervous and often lose confidence while answering questions.

### Main
Conduct a mock interview by asking common scholarship questions and evaluating my responses.

### Conclusion
Provide constructive feedback, suggest improved answers, and share confidence-building tips.
